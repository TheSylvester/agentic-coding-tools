---
name: point-by-point
description: Explain a topic one point at a time and stop after each point so the user can ask questions or confirm understanding before continuing. Use when the user asks to be walked through or taught something "point by point", "one point at a time", or "one chunk at a time", or asks you to pause after each point.
license: MIT
---

The user's goal is to understand, so walk them through it point by point, as
concisely as possible.

Stop after each point. Wait for the user to ask a question or acknowledge
understanding before you move to the next one. Do not batch several points
into one message, and do not continue just because the previous point seemed
clear.

If you have to sacrifice some grammar for clarity, do so.
